# Phase 6 — Validation · Functional Scope

**Goal:** Prove ATOM actually makes money on a drawdown-adjusted basis — the go-live blocker.

## In-scope functional modules
No new modules. This phase **exercises the whole system** (Phases 1–5) end-to-end on
paper/backtest and judges the result.

## Functional outcome of this phase
- A demonstrated, positive **drawdown-adjusted expectancy** over an agreed window.
- Survival behaviour confirmed: losers contained, equity curve survives.
- Evidence package the Board can use for a GO/NO-GO on real capital.

## Out of scope
- Live broker wiring + real money (Phase 7, post-GO).
